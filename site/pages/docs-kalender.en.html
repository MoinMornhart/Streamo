Calendar & watch plans

The Calendar tab shows a month grid with every day – the empty ones too. It contains four things: what you've planned, your watch plans, what's about to leave a platform and when new episodes air.

Watch plan – two episodes every Monday

On a show's detail page, 🔁 Watch plan sets on which days you watch it and how many episodes. Streamo ticks them off by itself on those days.

  • The plan does not remember where it is; it always takes the next unwatched episodes. Watch more in between and you never get an episode twice.
  • Specials and episodes that haven't aired are left out.
  • If the server was off on a plan day, it catches up – at most three sessions, so you don't come back from holiday to half a season ticked off.

With a time: appointments with a start and an end

Watch plans and watchlist dates can optionally get a time. The note then becomes a proper appointment – Streamo works out how long it takes from the runtime:

WhatLength
Watch planthe length of exactly the episodes up that evening – two 50-minute episodes make 20:15 to 21:55
Film on the watchlistthe film's runtime
Show on the watchlistone episode

If TMDB knows no runtime, Streamo assumes 45 minutes per episode or 120 per film. A watch plan with a time only ticks episodes off once the session is over – not from midnight on.

Subscribe in your own calendar

📅 Subscribe in the calendar tab shows the address for your calendar app. Set it up once and it keeps itself up to date – in your account's language.

AppHow
Apple Calendar (Mac, iPhone)Button Apple Calendar – or File → New Calendar Subscription
Google CalendarButton Google Calendar – or Other calendars → From URL
OutlookButton Outlook
Thunderbird & othersCopy the address and add it as a network calendar

Sessions with a time arrive with a real start and end, in your device's time zone; everything else is all-day.

The address is the only proof – a calendar app can't sign in. Don't share it. Create a new address makes old subscriptions stop working.

Streamo × ToDoch

ToDoch – the self-hosted to-do and calendar app – picks up this calendar automatically: copy the subscription address in Streamo and paste it in ToDoch under Areas → Add calendars. From then on every film you plan, every watch plan and every new episode shows up in your ToDoch calendar, synced in the background (every 15 minutes up to daily). Change things in Streamo – ToDoch follows.
