All your streaming subscriptions.
In one place. On your server.

Your own show and film database that tells you where every title streams and whether it's in your subscription – with a calendar, watch plans and statistics on which subscription pays off.

Install on Proxmox ⬇️ Windows app Read the docs

Installed in a few minutes

Run this in the shell of your Proxmox VE host – the installer creates a Debian container, installs everything and tells you the address at the end.

bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MoinMornhart/Streamo/main/scripts/streamo.sh)"

No Proxmox? The same installer runs on any Debian or Ubuntu system, including the Raspberry Pi. Go to the installation guide →

What's inside

📺

Where is it on?

Provider logos on every poster, outlined in green when your subscription covers it – split into subscription, free, rent and buy.

↗️

Straight to the provider

One click on Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ and a dozen or so more opens the service with a search for the title – no detour via JustWatch.

📚

Your library

Watchlist, per-episode progress, rating, favourites, notes. The status moves by itself from "want to watch" to "watched".

🗓️

Calendar you can subscribe to

Month grid with plans, watch plans and titles leaving soon – subscribe in Apple, Google or Outlook, in your account's language.

🔁

Watch plans with a time

"Two episodes every Monday at 8:15 pm": Streamo ticks them off, and the calendar shows the evening with a start and an end – worked out from the episode runtimes.

📊

Which subscription pays off?

Your library by provider, candidates for cancelling, and which extra subscription would unlock the most for you.

⏳

Leaving soon

If it's known until when a title stays on a provider, the tile says so: "5 days left", in red for the last three days.

🎬

Collections

Kingsman, Dune and co. in the right order, collapsible in the library – and shareable via a link, WhatsApp included.

✨

Discover

Top films of the week and of the year, suggestions from your own library with a reason, and a search that forgives typos.

👥

Several people

Own subscriptions, lists and region per person, invitations by link, friends – and what you can watch together.

🪟

Windows app

Its own window, tray, notifications about new episodes, search with Ctrl+Shift+S. Updates itself.

🌍

German or English

Everyone switches the interface language with one click – independent of the language titles and descriptions come in.

🎨

Your colour scheme

Your own accent colour and base tone per account, the same on every device. Looks like an app on your phone.

🔐

Secure sign-in

Password, passkey (Windows Hello, Face ID) and two-factor with an authenticator app. No credentials for third-party services.

🚀

Low maintenance

One command installs everything, update updates with a backup and automatic rollback. Availability is synced every hour.

What it looks like

On your phone too

The same interface, made for phones: the tabs move to the bottom, controls are big enough for a thumb, and added to the home screen Streamo looks like an app.

Where does the data come from?

Streamo does not sign in to Netflix and co. and needs no third-party credentials. You tick which subscriptions you have; availability comes from JustWatch via the free TMDB API. Both are matched – every hour, by themselves.
