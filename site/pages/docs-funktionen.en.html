All features

Streamo answers one question: Where can I watch this – and is it in my subscription? Everything else builds on that.

Connect providers

Under Providers you click the services you subscribe to – Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, WOW, Paramount+, Apple TV+, MagentaTV and more than a hundred others, depending on the region. Plan variants like "Netflix Standard with Ads" or "Paramount+ Amazon Channel" are merged into one service. Streamo needs no credentials for this: it only remembers which subscriptions you have.

Where is it on?

Every tile shows the provider logos right on the poster; outlined in green means "in your subscription". The detail page splits into subscription, free, rent and buy. Clicking a provider opens it directly – Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ and a dozen or so more with a search for the title; only unknown providers go through JustWatch.

Your library

Five states (Want to watch, Watching, Watched, Paused, Dropped), your own rating, favourites and notes. Tick off seasons and episodes one by one, a whole season or "everything up to here"; the status changes by itself. Filters and sorting live in the address, and the browser remembers the last ones used.

  • A date on the watchlist – optionally a day and a time when you want to watch something. The tile then says "Tomorrow" or "Friday, 20:15".
  • Leaving soon – if it's known until when a title stays on a provider, the tile says so: "5 days left", red at the end. Someone enters it by hand (TMDB has no end date) and it then applies to everyone on the instance.
  • Export – the whole library as JSON, and importable again.

Collections

Official collections like Kingsman appear by themselves; you put your own together – say, "what to watch before Spider-Man". In the library they can be grouped and collapsed. Every collection can be shared via a link, WhatsApp included; whoever opens it needs no account.

Discover

The start page shows the top films of the week and of the year (from 500 votes up), and above them "For you" – suggestions based on what you've watched and rated well, each with a reason. The search forgives typos: "Kingsmann" finds Kingsman, "spiderman" finds Spider-Man.

Statistics

How are your titles spread across your subscriptions? Which one has nothing from your list right now? Which extra subscription would unlock the most – and how many hours have you already put in?

Several people and friends

Everyone has their own subscriptions, library, progress and region. New accounts are created via invitation links. Friends see each other's lists, and Streamo works out what you can watch together – based on both your subscriptions. Administrators manage rights and accounts under Settings → Users.

Appearance and language

Everyone picks their accent colour (eight presets or free) and a base tone – dark blue or true black. Switch the interface language in the header with 🌐 DE | EN; the language of titles and descriptions is set separately.

Automatic sync

Every hour Streamo checks where the titles in all libraries are streaming now – about 20 seconds of work for 300 titles. Set the interval under Settings → Sync, from hourly to "never".
